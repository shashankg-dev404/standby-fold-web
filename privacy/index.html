Privacy Policy

StandBy Fold: Always-On Clock  |  Effective date: October 14, 2026

StandBy Fold ("we", "our", or "the app") turns a Samsung Galaxy Z Fold into a bedside or desk clock: when the phone is locked and folded into a tent, the outer screen shows a clock, widgets, photos or what is playing. This policy explains what the app reads, what (little) leaves your phone, and why.

In short: the app has no accounts, no ads and no advertising ID. Everything it reads stays on your phone, except a weather request (if you use weather), anonymous crash reports, and a small set of anonymous usage events. Nothing at all is sent while the board is on your screen at night.

2. What leaves your phone

2.1 Weather (optional)

If you use weather, the app asks MET Norway, the Norwegian Meteorological Institute, for a forecast. The request carries an approximate location: either the place you typed, or your coarse (not precise) location cut to four decimal places, as MET's terms ask. It also carries the app's version and our contact address, which MET requires of every client. MET's own privacy terms apply to that request. If you name your location automatically, Android's built-in place-name service (part of Google Play services) may receive the same approximate coordinates to return the town name. The app never asks for precise or background location.

2.2 Crash reports

If the app crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent through Google's Firebase Crashlytics: the stack trace, the app version, the Android version, the device model and a random installation identifier. It contains no name, no account, no location, no calendar, photo or notification content.

2.3 Anonymous usage events

The app records a short, fixed list of anonymous events (for example: the app was set up, a setting was changed, the board was shown or paused) and sends them to our own server on Cloudflare Workers. Each event carries only its name, simple values such as a setting's choice or a counted bucket, the app version, the device model and a random installation identifier created on your phone. No events are recorded while the board is showing.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, names, email addresses, phone numbers or contacts.
  • No advertising ID, no ads, no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No precise or background location.
  • No photo, calendar or notification content ever leaves your phone.
  • No microphone, camera or audio recording.
  • We do not sell, rent or share data with anyone for marketing.

5. Retention and deletion

  • On your phone: everything is deleted when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.
  • Crash reports: kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Anonymous usage events: about 30 days, then purged automatically.

Because the crash reports and events are anonymous, we cannot link them to you. Uninstalling the app stops all collection at once; the remaining records expire on the schedule above. For any question about your data, email us.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS. Data on the phone is kept in the app's private storage.
