StandBy Fold: Always-On Clock

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Email neko.app.contact@gmail.com and include your phone model and Android version. We read every message.

How it works

Lock your Fold, fold it into a tent at any angle that keeps it standing, and let go. After three seconds of stillness the clock appears on the outer screen. No charger, no calibration. Pick it up and it goes away.

The board does not appear

  • Open the app and check that watching is on (the notification says "watching").
  • Open Help in the app: every check there should be green. Grant anything it marks.
  • Battery Saver, a call, quiet hours or a low battery keep the board off by design; the app's settings let you change the last two.
  • Samsung's battery settings: set the app to Unrestricted so Android does not stop it.

Pausing

Pause from the notification, the Quick Settings tile, or inside the app.

Supported phones

Samsung Galaxy Z Fold phones running Android 14 or newer.
